Fleet Registry

Every ship in the game, drawn from the cells the game itself meshes. The stock hulls come off assets/hulls/*.ftvx; the four alien archetypes are generated from a seed and exist nowhere else a person can open. Nothing on this page is typed beside a class: every number, and every navy's swatch, is measured off the model.

The ladder

Four navies, four rungs each

A rung is a CELL SIZE and not a longer profile: the lattice is 32 by 32 by 64 for every warship, and what changes is what one cell is worth in the world. A corvette is a short profile at the frigate's cell, a destroyer is the escort cell, a heavy cruiser the cruiser cell. One hull is on nobody's ladder: the Terran Fleet Carrier stands at the capital cell, twice a heavy cruiser's, which by the cube rule is eight times its mass and eight times its hull.

The civil yards

Seven trades, and what the retreat gives each one to do

The civil hulls do not build a ladder, they build trades. Six of the seven fly a support role in The Long Retreat; the container ship has no job yet and is here because it is in the fleet.

The swarm

Four archetypes, seeded and mirrored

A mote is built on a 16 cubed lattice (24 for a mother) from a seed and nothing else, mirrored about x, every limb a walk of one face steps from a cell already there. Two of the four are fielded today.

What the numbers mean

The rules that turn a hull into a ship

Only the reactor kills it

REACTOR_LOSS is half. A hull can be shot to pieces everywhere else and keep flying, which is what makes a wreck that is still fighting possible at all.

The reactor is DERIVED rather than authored: it is the ball of solid cells around the most buried cell in the ship. Buried in the tables is how many courses of material a shot would have to cross to reach it from outside space.

Plating is soft again

ARMOUR is 1.0 for the fleet and 8.0 for a carrier. A hundred times the bare material made a ship that could not be hurt; what actually needed fixing was the death rule, and the reactor rule is what lets the plating be soft.

Hit points is the sum over every cell, so it is a measure of how much material there is rather than of how long the ship lives.

Guns are read off the ship

guns_of clusters the cells the export says are gunnery and puts a muzzle at each cluster's outermost cell. Nothing about a gun is authored beside the hull, so a class with no weapons has none rather than having some invented for it.

Every gun is a turret: its cells are lifted out of the hull before the bricks are built and it pivots on its own cluster's middle.

Two radii, two questions

Radius is the bounding sphere, to the furthest corner of the furthest cell: the right answer to "could this be hit". Volume radius is the sphere with the same volume as the solid cells: the right answer to "how big is this".

Using the first as the second once put the swarm in orbit round empty space beside every asteroid.

A jump is priced by the fleet

The drive costs 250 to spin up and every other hull standing inside the field adds its own rung: 25 for a corvette, 50 for a frigate or a civil trade, 100 for a destroyer, 200 for a cruiser.

So the ship that helps you hold a system is the ship you then pay to take out of it.

Who flies what

The flagship is whatever class the setup form picked. An escort is a COPY of the flagship's class, up to six. A fighter is a terran_corvette whatever the flagship is. A support ship flies the civil trade that would actually do its job.

Carriers are motherships, and they go on the same reactor rule the fleet does.

Keeping this current

This page is the record of every ship in the game. When a ship is added or removed, when a hull is re-exported, or when anything on it moves (stats, guns, the reactor, a role, what a class is for), the registry is regenerated and republished in the same change.

Nothing here is hand maintained, which is what makes that cheap: the exporter reads the hull directory rather than a list, so a class added tomorrow is on this page tomorrow, and the numbers cannot drift from the models because they are measured off them at export time.

Regenerate the geometry and the index, then republish this page:
